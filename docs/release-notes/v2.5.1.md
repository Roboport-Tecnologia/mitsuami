## Windows (WinUI)

- **A new window reports no frame until the app has sized it, given it a frame or shown it**, because its first resize came while it was still where and as large as Windows makes a new one, down its cascade, and the app's own frame, applied next, never replaced it: an app that kept the frame kept the cascade's, and a window it reopened maximized un-maximized there.
- **A window restored from maximized reports its restored size with its restored position**, because the position came before XAML had laid the content out at the restored size, and the two were reported together, the frame keeping the maximized size.
